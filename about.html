About Know Patience Fragance

Know Patience Fragrance is a quiet stand against vulgar institutional luxury and uninspired copies of popular scents.

The elegant fine fragrances offered here are crafted in small batches — each one painstakingly designed to evoke confident presence, not noise.

We believe scents are the bridge connecting memories with emotions and smell is intimate and personal.

Olfactory elegance is never offensive or abrasive.

Based in the United States, we work with those who value timelessness over trends, and individuality over imitation.
